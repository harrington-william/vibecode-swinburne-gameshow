1. 🇻🇳 Vietnam — Tet Holiday

During Tet, the Vietnamese Lunar New Year, families often gather for a special meal. One famous dish is a square sticky rice cake filled with mung beans and pork and wrapped in green leaves.

What is this traditional food called?

A. Pho
B. Banh Chung
C. Banh Mi
D. Goi Cuon

Answer: B. Banh Chung

Explanation: Banh Chung is one of the most iconic foods of Vietnamese Tet.

💡 Hint: Square shape + sticky rice + Tet = 🇻🇳

2. 🇹🇭 Thailand — Songkran

8. 🇪🇸 Spain — New Year

In Spain, people traditionally eat 12 grapes at midnight on New Year’s Eve, with each grape representing one month of the coming year.

True or False?

Answer: TRUE ✅

Explanation: The tradition is known as the 12 grapes of luck, and people eat one grape with each clock chime at midnight.

💡 Hint: 12 months = 12 grapes. 🍇

9. 🇮🇹 Italy — Christmas

In Italy, panettone, a tall sweet bread containing dried and candied fruit, is traditionally associated with Easter celebrations.

True or False?

Answer: FALSE ❌

Explanation: Panettone is traditionally associated with Christmas, not Easter. It is one of Italy’s most famous Christmas foods.

💡 Hint: Italy + Christmas + sweet bread. 🎄

10. 🇫🇷 France — Epiphany

During Epiphany in France, people traditionally eat galette des rois, a pastry that contains a small hidden object. The person who finds it becomes the king or queen of the celebration.

12. 🇬🇧 United Kingdom — Christmas

In the United Kingdom, Christmas pudding is traditionally served during Christmas. It is usually a light, creamy dessert similar to custard.

True or False?

Answer: FALSE ❌

Explanation: Christmas pudding is actually a rich steamed dessert, usually containing dried fruit and spices. It is not a light, creamy custard.

13. 🇲🇽 Mexico — Día de los Muertos
In Mexico, Día de los Muertos is a colorful celebration that honors deceased family members. A traditional sweet bread, often decorated with shapes representing bones, is placed on family altars and shared during the festival.

This bread is called __________.

Answer: Pan de Muerto

Explanation: Pan de muerto means “bread of the dead” and is strongly associated with the festival.

💡 Hint: If you’ve watched Pixar’s Coco, you have probably seen this celebration. 💀🌼
